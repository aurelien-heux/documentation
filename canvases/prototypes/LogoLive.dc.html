The toolbox prompt

A toolbox silhouette (body + handle) holding a terminal prompt. Alignment grid: the prompt sits on one baseline and is centred in the box; in the horizontal lockup the capitals of “AIToolbox” are as tall as the box and centred vertically on the whole mark (badge top to box bottom); in the stacked lockup the mark and the wordmark are both centred on the vertical axis. The AI badge rises above the box line beside the handle. The chevron is the “dev”, the box is the “toolbox”, and the neon-green cursor is the one accent that survives on every background. The box is night — the palette's seed #0D0B14 itself (logo-box), with a white chevron and the full neon cursor; on dark backgrounds it turns lavender (primary tone 80) with a night chevron and an emerald cursor. The wordmark keeps “Toolbox” in deep violet. Built on the same 4 px grid as the UI; corners follow the MD3 Large shape.
