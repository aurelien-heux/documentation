Brand · Motion · progress indicators · final

Progress, in the Constructor family

Now that the brand loader is the AI drawing the toolbox, the in-screen indicators can speak the same language instead of stock Material: a teal pen that leads, dashed construction guides, violet for what is done. Roles and sizes stay the MD3 ones so nothing changes in the Angular mapping — `mat-progress-spinner` and `mat-progress-bar` keep their slots, these are their skins. Four pieces, light and dark.

P1 · Pen orbit (circular)The circular indicator as a pen trip: a violet arc with the teal pen at its head runs around a faint track. Same sizes and roles as MD3 (48 content · 24 inline · 18 in a button); it is the loader’s little sibling instead of a generic spinner.
P2 · Guide line (linear)The track becomes a dashed construction guide; the violet bar runs along it with the pen at its leading edge. Determinate: violet up to the value, pen on the edge, dashed guide for what is left. Top of the app bar, cards, table loading rows.

P3 · Constructor determinateFor long operations with a known value (install, deploy, migration): the pen draws the box in proportion to progress — 0 → 35 → 70 → 100 % — and at 100 % the toolbox fills and the prompt appears. The last tile animates through the cycle. 64–96 px, dialogs and empty states.
P4 · Working dots, two meaningsThe three dots stay, but their colour says who is working: green (`tertiary`) when a command executes, teal (`ai`) when a model is thinking. Same 1.4 s rhythm as today; inline after a log line or a chat message.
