AIToolbox · components · tooltip · proposal Tooltips Material 3 plain and rich tooltips dressed in the AIToolbox roles, plus three content rules the platform needs every day: full value of truncated data, the cause of a status, the reason an action is disabled. Calm chrome (inverse surface, small type), loud only when the content is AI or a status.

Pick the format
AAn icon button without a visible label.
BSame, and the action has a shortcut.
CText is cut with an ellipsis and the full value matters.
DA status needs its cause or its time.
EA control is disabled.
FA term needs a sentence or a link to explain it.
GContent was produced by AI.
—Anything longer, or a form the user must fill: a menu, a side sheet or a dialog, not a tooltip.

PlacementPlain: centred on the anchor, 8 px away; below in the header and the top 120 px of the screen, above elsewhere; flips on collision and keeps 8 px from the viewport edge (then aligns start or end). Rich: below-start of its info icon, same flip rule.
MotionEnter: fade + scale .8 → 1 from the anchor side, 150 ms, emphasized decelerate. Exit: fade, 75 ms. Reduced motion: fade only.
Accessibilityrole="tooltip", linked with aria-describedby; opens on keyboard focus too; Esc closes without moving focus; hovering the tooltip keeps it open (WCAG 1.4.13). Rich tooltips with actions open on click and take focus.
TouchNo hover: plain tooltips show on long-press for 1.5 s; rich tooltips open on tap of the info icon and close on tap outside; data tooltips become a “Copied” snackbar.
Angular MaterialmatTooltip for A–E with matTooltipShowDelay=500, matTooltipHideDelay=150, matTooltipPosition="below|above", matTooltipClass = ait-tip-data / ait-tip-status. F–G: an ait-rich-tooltip component in a cdkConnectedOverlay.
Do
• Label every icon-only button (A or B), even if it also has an aria-label.
• Sentence case, no final period on plain tooltips; one idea per tooltip.
• Give the full value of truncated data (C) and make it copyable.
• Explain disabled actions (E) and say how to unlock them.
Don’t
• Repeat a visible label (“Run” on a Run button).
• Put links, buttons or form fields in a plain tooltip — use a rich one or a menu.
• Hide information that exists nowhere else; touch users may never see it.
• Use teal for a non-AI tooltip, or a status colour without its symbol.
