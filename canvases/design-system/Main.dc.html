Color roles

Tokens 5.0 · the Sweep palette: the three Sweep colours seed the scheme. The Sweep purple #39235F is the light primary (tone 20, hue 303; 12.7:1 on the page); the dark primary family stays on the lavender #C4C0FF. The pale purple #DED7EB seeds the secondary and is the light secondary-container (tonal buttons, chips, the active navigation pill). The lime #DCEAB4 seeds the tertiary: tertiary-container is the Run button, lime in both themes with a Sweep purple label; tertiary (olive in light, pale lime in dark) marks running and deploy states. One tone rule: light roles at tone 30, dark roles at 80, containers 90 / 30, text on containers 10 / 90. Night neutrals from #0D0B14 (hue 296): near-neutral page and cards in light, light-purple containers, the navigation frame on the violet band nav-container #EDE4FF. Extended: ai (deep teal in light, light teal in dark), success, warning. The brand group logo-* (night box, neon cursor, teal AI badge) is for the logo only. Shape: 16 px is the ceiling on every rectangle, full is for circles.
