Switch

Turns one thing on or off, and the change takes effect at once. Two sizes in the same roles: Standard for settings pages and forms, Compact for dense lists and menus (the assistant's Tools · Skills · MCP servers menu). The whole row is the control; the switch is its state.

Skills
Switch or checkbox
  • Switch when the change applies at once, with no Save: a tool on or off, auto-deploy, notifications.
  • Checkbox when the choice is confirmed later by a button, or picks several items: form fields, filters, rows to act on.
  • Never a checkbox in the assistant's tools menu, a settings list or a profile on/off (Feedback admin).
  • A switch that needs a confirmation (it deploys, deletes, costs) asks in a dialog and only flips after Confirm.
Writing and layout
  • Label on the left, switch on the right, aligned on the row's end; the whole row is the hit target.
  • The label names the thing that is on, not the action: "Auto-deploy on merge", not "Enable auto-deploy".
  • No "On" / "Off" text beside the switch; a summary may count them ("Tools · 3 on").
  • One line of supporting text at most, in body-small, under the label.
  • Standard in pages and dialogs; Compact only in menus, panes and tables where rows are 44 px.
Accessibility and markup 
<button type="button" class="sw-row" role="switch"

  <span class="tgl" aria-hidden="true">
    <span class="tgl-t"></span></span>
</button>
  • Space and Enter toggle; the screen reader says "switch, on". The visual switch is aria-hidden.
  • Target at least 44 × 44 (the row). Contrast against the surface: track on 10.7 : 1, outline off 4.3 : 1 (3 : 1 is the floor for controls).
