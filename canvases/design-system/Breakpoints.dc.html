The content follows the page area
KPI cards
1 per row under 300 px · 2 per row from 300 px (a 360 px phone) · 4 per row from 720 px of page area.
Side column (AI suggestion, alerts, deploy window)
Beside the table, 360 px (400 px from 1440 px), when the page area is ≥ 1040 px; otherwise stacked full width: alerts above the table, the other cards under it.
Data tables
Under 600 px the rows become a two-line list (state mark + name, a meta line, actions in the row menu); 600–799 px only priority-1 and -2 columns + the row menu; from 800 px every column. A table that must keep every column scrolls inside its own frame with the first column sticky — the page itself never scrolls sideways.
Forms
One column, 600 px max; pairs (start / end date) side by side from 600 px of page area; actions at the end, full width under 600 px.
Tabs
Scrollable under 600 px of page area (counts kept), never wrapped.
Reading pages (docs, settings, policies)
Text column 840 px max, centred in the page area; dashboards and tables use the full width.
Height. Under 480 px (a phone in landscape): rail instead of the bar, top bar scrolls away, forms full screen. Touch. 48 px targets under (pointer: coarse) at every width. Test at 320 (WCAG reflow, 1280 px at 400 % zoom), 360, 600, 768, 840, 1024, 1200, 1440, 1600, 1920 px, and 740 × 360 (phone in landscape); at 320 px the page never scrolls sideways (WCAG 1.4.10).
