Adopted · DChangelog
Each change is a row: tag, what changed, detail. Versions and changes stay scannable down the feed, and a release with many small changes keeps its height. Actions sit on the line under the table, with Useful? at its end; the item ends with the Discussion panel. Applied to the live Community prototype.
Below 840 px a row puts the tag and the title on one line and the detail under them; the actions and the feedback wrap as two groups.
Same in every layout Meta: version, type (sparkle only on AI topics), action-required tile, date · areas, unread. Then title, summary (72 ch max), tagged changes, actions (Read, Try it, Watch, Acknowledge), useful?, then the Discussion expansion panel shared with Ideas: summary bar as header (threads, messages, who took part, last message); open, threads by three with Show more, each an opening message with its replies (the last two shown, earlier ones on demand), role chips for the platform team, champions and authors, Reply, and the new-message field. Side column, in this order: champions, office hours, rating. The get-started checklist is gone: a release proposes its own first step ("Try it in the app").
