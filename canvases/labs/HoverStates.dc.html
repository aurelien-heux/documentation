AIToolbox · state layers · hover · B adopted Hover states: alternatives to the grey Every component is shown in its hover state, on the surface it really sits on. Today the hover is a grey layer (on-surface 8 %), which reads dark and dirty on white tables and on the pale-purple menus and navigation. Coloured components (last four rows) keep their own colour as the layer in every variant. Live version: the Hover States artifact.

Recommendation

Adopted: B · Violet tint for every neutral surface. It is lighter than today’s grey on white (#F3F3F7 instead of #EDEDED), it stays in the hue of the pale-purple containers and the violet navigation instead of greying them, and it is one rule (primary at 6 %, pressed 10 %), so Angular Material only needs its state-layer colour changed. Coloured components (filled, tonal, Run, AI chip, active navigation) keep their own content colour as the layer, as Material asks. D · Container step is the alternative if you prefer no transparency at all; it needs one token per surface.
