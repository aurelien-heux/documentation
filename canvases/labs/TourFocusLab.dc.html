Decision · D adoptedSpotlight
The scrim does the work: everything else drops to 42 % and the target stays at full brightness, so the eye lands on it before reading a word. The ring marks the exact edge of the target. The scrim fades in over 350 ms and the card rises 8 px; nothing loops. The page under the scrim is not clickable, only the target is, which also prevents mis-clicks during a tour. Applied to the Community prototype (all tours) and documented in the design system as the GuidedTour component.
Fallbacks. Reduced motion: the spotlight appears without the fade. Targets wider than 60 % of the window (a whole table): soft scrim at 16 % instead of 42 %, so the content stays readable. A target in the rail or header: the cutout follows the 16 px item radius.
Design-system notes Scrim: the `scrim` role at 42 % (dialogs use 32 %); cutout radius 12 px, or 16 px on navigation items. Ring: 2 px `primary`, 4 px gap at 18 %. Card: `surface-container-high`, level 2, 12 px radius, 340 px, placed beside the target (right, else left, else below). Targets are `data-anchor` elements measured at runtime, scrolled into view first. Never teal: the tour is chrome, not an AI feature. Never the beacon dot alone as the only cue (colour alone): the card always names the target. Motion: MD emphasized easing, 350–700 ms, at most one looping element per screen (the beacon loop stops when the card closes).
