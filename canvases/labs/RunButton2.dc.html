AIToolbox · the Run action · round 2 · E adopted, then replaced by round 3 · L2 (2026-11-14) Run without the green Today Run is the filled tertiary: emerald with white in light — 6.5 : 1, under the system’s 7 : 1 rule — and the full neon #00F29E in dark, brighter than anything else on a night screen. Round 2 looks beyond green: a neutral “terminal” button from the logo, and three new hues placed away from the colours that already mean something (violet primary, teal AI, success green, amber warning, red error). Live version: the Run Button Colours artifact.

Recommendation

Adopted: E · Night terminal. It turns Run into the brand’s own gesture: the night toolbox with the neon cursor, as in the logo. It is the strongest contrast of all (19.5 : 1 / 15.1 : 1), it never collides with a status colour (green “Passed”, amber, red) or with the violet primary, and green keeps a single, precise job: the play icon. Row actions become text buttons in on-surface with the same green play icon. H · Lime is the option if Run must stay a colour: complementary to the violet and clearly separate from the success green. Azure (F) and magenta (G) work technically but blur with the primary violet and the error red respectively.
