Assessment

Adopted: L2 · lime with a purple edge (2026-11-14). Lime is a clear “go” and fits the Sweep identity; with Sweep purple the label reaches 10.4 : 1 in both themes. The plain lime fill (L1) is too pale for a light page — 1.2 : 1 on the page and 1.1 : 1 in a dialog — so it reads as a chip, not as the main action; the 1 px Sweep purple edge (L2) fixes that. Two risks to look at in the row-action line: the pale lime sits close to the Passed green chip, so Re-run keeps lime only in a small disc behind the play icon; and Sweep purple is nearly the primary violet (#39235F vs #35307E), so L3 makes Run look like any primary button in light. L4 (the whole Sweep palette) changes little on screen because the purples are close to ours: worth it as a Sweep feature accent, not as a system-wide swap. Compared with today’s night terminal (E), lime is softer and more on-brand for Sweep, but E stays the stronger, more neutral action across all features.
