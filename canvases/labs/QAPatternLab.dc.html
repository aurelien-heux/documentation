Adopted · A + Show moreSummary bar
One full-width bar, always at the end of the item, is both the summary and the only control, in What’s new and in Ideas alike. Closed, it already answers “is there anything for me?” (count, who asked, answered and waiting, last answer); open, it stays where it was, above the thread, and closes it again. Applied to the live Community prototype and to both layout labs.
Show more. The bar is the header of one expansion panel; open, the panel shows three questions, newest first. The link “Show 3 more questions” adds the next three under them, says where you are (“3 of 7 shown”), moves the focus to the first new question and announces it. Closing the panel goes back to the first three.
One set of Q&A metadata, everywhere Summary (item level): Questions · count, up to three askers, “n answered” and “n waiting” StatusChips (only when not zero), “Last answer 2 days ago” (or “No answer yet”). Compact summary (lists, admin, notifications): “3 questions · 1 waiting”, the same words in the Ideas list rows, the Administration triage and the release list. Question: asker, date, question, Waiting for an answer. Answer: Answered, who, role chip (Platform team, Champion, Author), date, text. Every control: `aria-expanded` + `aria-controls`, state shown by the chevron and the selected look (never by the label alone), Enter and Space, focus kept on the control.
