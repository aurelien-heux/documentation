Colour system review

Tokens 4.7 works and passes WCAG AA, but it grew one decision at a time: a lime Run, a Sweep primary, a violet band, a teal AI. This lab counts what that left behind, then shows four ways forward on the same screen, in light and dark, next to today. Pick an option below; everything under it follows.

1 · What is wrong today

Ten findings from tokens.json 4.7, both Angular theme files and the canvas boards.

01

Two purples for one brand

Since 4.7 the light primary is on the Sweep hue 303; every other purple role and the whole dark theme stay on 288.

Six tokens for one button

Same value, different names

05

Navigation has its own pair

06

No single tone rule

Light roles: primary 20, tertiary and error 30, secondary, success and warning 35, ai 80 with ai-strong 40 beside it.

#39235Ft20#005232t30#1B5F1Ft35#80D5D4ai t80#006A6Aai-strong t40
07

AI upside down

ai is the pale teal in both themes, so dots, pen and guide on light need a second token, ai-strong. Material would make ai the strong one.

#80D5D4ai#006A6Aai-strong
08

Brand inside the UI scheme

logo-box, on-logo-box, logo-cursor (and ai as the badge) are listed with the UI roles; the legacy surface-variant is still exported.

#0D0B14logo-box#00F29Elogo-cursor#E6E0ECsurface-variant
09

Values outside the tokens

theme.m2.scss holds 44 hex values that are not tokens (palettes, hover layers worked out by hand); the canvas boards use 268 distinct values, 197 of them not tokens (2,931 uses); the feature-marks package still has #35307E.

10

Exceptions kept for contrast

Light accents sit darker than Material (30 instead of 40) and the dark inverse-primary at 30: deliberate, better written as one rule than as exceptions.

Recommendation · C · Sweep palette

C, with today's lime Run kept in dark. It removes the special cases instead of renaming them. The Sweep purple seeds every purple role (one hue, not two), the pale purple becomes the secondary, the lime becomes the tertiary, and the Run button is simply the tertiary container. That is 46 tokens instead of 58 and 3 exceptions instead of 11, and every text pair stays at 7.2 : 1 or more. B is the safe fallback if nothing on screen may move. D shows what going fully generic costs: the Sweep purple, the lime and the violet band all leave the UI.

Decision to take

Pick A, B, C or D. If C, settle the three points under 'To settle if you pick it': the lime Run in dark (recommended: keep it, one pin), olive and pale-lime running dots instead of the neon, and the active pill at 1.1 : 1 carried by the 3 px bar. Nothing changes until you choose. Then tokens 5.0, both theme files, the canvas boards, the assets and the project docs move together in one pass.
