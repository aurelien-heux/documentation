Adopted · BAdmin sidebar
One page with a 240 px sidebar: Core, then one group per feature module, each group being what the remote module declares in its manifest. Every section is one click away and the current one is always visible; rights simply remove groups and items (a Sweep admin sees Sweep, and Feedback · Sweep under Community). New feature modules add a group; nothing else moves. Below 1200 px the sidebar becomes a grouped Section select above the content. Applied to the live Community prototype.
Second choice: D, the console, if Administration becomes a place people stay in for long sessions: the admin tree takes the rail’s place and the page gets the full width, at the cost of leaving the app’s navigation.
A keeps a hub that is pleasant to land on but adds a click to every section and a back chip to every page; C needs two rows (scope tabs, section chips) and the scope tabs scroll on phones.
Same in every shell Core (platform admins only): General (landing), Users and permissions, Groups, Audit log. Accounts come from the company directory (SSO), so Users and permissions only gives admin rights: filters All, Admins, Requests, search, the user table (admin rights, team, last active) and an Admin rights panel (Platform admin, Sweep admin, Community admin, Feedback · Sweep; approve or decline a request). Sweep (remote module sweep-admin@1.4): Connectors. Community (community-admin@4.6): Releases, Tours, Feedback, Ideas triage, Insights. Feature pages carry the Remote module line (id, owner, scope). Header context: “Administration · Core” over the section, or “Administration · Sweep” over the section. Administration stays the last rail destination; the rail’s own active item is Admin.
