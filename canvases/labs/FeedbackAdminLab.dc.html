Adopted · ACommunity › Feedback
The platform team reads the inbox, so feedback stays in Community, next to ideas, releases and discussions: listen, decide, ship, announce in one module. Every profile is edited in Community › Feedback; a feature admin gets the scoped right Feedback · <feature>, separate from Community admin, and sees only their profile, their inbox and the cap (read-only). Applied to the live Community prototype.
Kept reversible. FeedbackProfile stays a component of its own and the right is per feature, so moving to B later (a dedicated team takes the inbox, or an Overview across features is needed) is moving one group in the sidebar. 1 fits if feature teams one day own their feedback end to end.
Same in every variant The FeedbackProfile editor (entry points, up to 3 questions, sampling, routing, options, live preview), one inbox with reply / route to Jira / propose as idea (with the author’s consent), surveys, and one solicitation cap per user set by the platform team. Bugs go to Jira, never to Ideas.
