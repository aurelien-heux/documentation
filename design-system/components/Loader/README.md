# Loader

Two kinds of waiting: the brand loader for app-level waits, Material indicators for everything inside a screen.

- **Brand loader ("Constructor")** — the AI builds the toolbox: the AI tag is introduced first (the teal tile grows on its corner, "A" then "I" rise into it, a light sheen crosses it), then dashed construction guides appear, a teal pen leaves the tag and draws the box, handle, prompt and cursor in one trip, goes home, and the night toolbox becomes solid while the tag pulses once; 4.8 s loop restarting with a clean cut. Add `ait-once` on the SVG root to play once and stay on the logo (splash intro). Its viewBox is `-4 2 76 64`, so size it by height (96 px tall = 114 px wide); use 96 px for splash and workspace boot, 40 px minimum. Colours are `primary`, `on-primary`, `tertiary-container`, `ai` and `on-ai`, so it follows the theme.
- **Pen orbit (circular)** — the circular indicator in the Constructor family: a violet arc with the teal pen at its head runs around a faint `primary-container` track. 48 px in content, 24 px inline, 18 px inside a button.
- **Guide line (linear)** — 4 px: the track is a dashed teal construction guide, the violet bar runs along it with the pen at its leading edge; determinate = violet up to the value, pen on the edge, dashes for the rest. Top of the app bar during navigation, cards, table loading rows.
- **Constructor determinate** — long operations with a known value (install, deploy, migration): the pen draws the box in proportion to progress (`--p`, 0–100) and at 100 % the toolbox fills and the prompt appears. 64–96 px in dialogs and empty states.
- **Working dots** — three dots, 1.4 s rhythm; the colour says who is working: green (`tertiary`) when a command executes, teal (`ai`) when a model is thinking. Inline after a log line or a chat message.
- All respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: the brand loader and the determinate show the finished logo, the orbit, bars and dots stop, and the label carries the state.
- **Keyboard and screen reader:** the loader is `role="status"` with a visible or hidden label; under reduced motion it shows the finished mark.
- **Angular Material:** `mat-progress-spinner` / `mat-progress-bar` keep their slots and APIs; the pen orbit and guide line are their skins (inline SVG from `loading-states/` + `loading-states.css`, or a thin wrapper component around the Material ones for the value binding). The brand loader is the inline SVG from `assets/Logos` (`loading-states/`), app and workspace start only — never a `mat-*` component. Full map and theme files: `angular-material.md`.
