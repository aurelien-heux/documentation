# Breakpoints

The five Material 3 window size classes and what the layout does in each. A breakpoint is the lower bound of a class: a 600 px window is Medium.

- **Compact** under 600 px · **Medium** 600–839 · **Expanded** 840–1199 · **Large** 1200–1599 (the reference mockups, 1440 × 900) · **Extra-large** from 1600. Mobile-first `min-width` queries on the window width, never on the device.
- **The shell follows the window.** Navigation: NavigationBar under 600 px; the rail by default from 600 px — its menu button opens the drawer on demand, over the page up to 1199 px, standard 300 px (pushes the page) from 1200 px. Header: 56 px with search as an icon under 600 px; search field 240 / 320 – 440 px with the Ask chip as an icon up to 1199 px; 440 px search, labelled Ask chip and user chip with name and role from 1200 px. Assistant: full screen under 600 px, in place of the page 600–1199 px, docked 400 px from 1200 px beside the rail (an open drawer folds back to the rail so the page keeps ≥ 720 px).
- **The content follows the page area** (between the page margins), because the rail (or the open drawer) and the docked assistant take part of the window: KPI cards 1 / 2 / 4 per row at 0 / 300 / 720 px; side column beside the table from 1040 px (400 px wide from 1380 px), stacked below that; tables become lists under 600 px, keep priority-1 and -2 columns up to 799 px, every column from 800 px.
- **Margins and grid:** 16 px · 4 columns · 16 px gutters (Compact), 24 · 8 · 24 (Medium), 24 · 12 · 24 (Expanded), 32 · 12 · 24 (Large and up). Reading pages cap the text at 840 px, forms at 600 px.
- **Overlays:** under 600 px forms open as full-screen dialogs, side sheets become bottom sheets, the primary action becomes an extended FAB above the NavigationBar and snackbars span the width above it. Side sheets are modal up to 1199 px, standard from 1200 px.
- **Height and touch:** under 480 px of height (phone in landscape) the rail replaces the NavigationBar and forms go full screen; 48 px targets under `(pointer: coarse)` at every width.
- **Keyboard and screen reader:** a layout change never moves focus or loses it; the navigation keeps the same landmarks (`nav aria-label="Primary"`) and the same order in every class; at 320 px (1280 px at 400 % zoom) nothing is lost and the page never scrolls sideways (WCAG 1.4.10) — only a table or a code block scrolls, in its own frame.
- **Angular Material:** `angular/breakpoints.ts` — `AitWindow` queries for `BreakpointObserver` (not the CDK `Breakpoints` constants, which are 600 / 960 / 1280 / 1920) and the `AitWindowClass` service: `windowClass()`, `navigation()` (`bar` | `rail` | `drawer`, rail by default), `drawerOpen()` / `openDrawer()` / `closeDrawer()`, `drawerMode()` for `mat-sidenav [mode]`, `assistantMode()`, `fullScreenDialogs()`. Sass: `@use 'breakpoints' as bp;` with `bp.from()`, `bp.below()`, `bp.only()`, `bp.short`, `bp.page-from()`. CSS: `--ait-page-margin`, `--ait-grid-columns`, `--ait-grid-gutter`, `.ait-page`, `.ait-kpis`, `.ait-split`, `.ait-col-p2 / -p3` in `css/tokens.css`.
