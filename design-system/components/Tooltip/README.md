# Tooltip

Two families, as in Material 3: **plain** (inverse surface, short, never interactive) and **rich** (container surface, can hold actions). C–E are plain tooltips with a content rule, not new components. No new colours: every value is a token. Spec board: `mockups/22-tooltips`; CSS: `brand/css/tooltips.css`.

- **A · Plain** — names an icon-only control. `inverse-surface` / `inverse-on-surface`, label-medium (BNPP Type 500, 12/16), min-height 24, padding 4 × 8, `radius-xs` 4, max-width 240, one line (two at most), no caret, sentence case, no final period.
- **B · Plain + shortcut** — the same with the keyboard shortcut after the label: keys in Open Sans 600 11/16, 1 px border in `inverse-on-surface` at 33 %, radius 4, 8 px after the label; ⌘ on macOS, Ctrl elsewhere.
- **C · Data** — the full value of a truncated identifier (run ID, commit, path, secret name): value in Open Sans 600, breaks anywhere, max-width 320; second line “Click to copy” with its icon → “Copied” with a check in `primary-container` for 1.5 s. The truncated anchor carries a dotted `outline` underline.
- **D · Status** — the cause and time of a status chip or health dot: a 16 px state tile (the status `*-container` with its `on-*-container` symbol — never colour alone), the status phrase, then the facts in Open Sans 600 (“Degraded since 10:24 · 3 of 5 health checks failing”).
- **E · Disabled reason** — why an action is unavailable and how to unlock it, two lines max, ending with the way out (“Ask a platform admin.”). The control uses `aria-disabled` and stays focusable so keyboard users get the tooltip.
- **F · Rich** — explains a term next to a label, field or metric, opened from an info icon: `surface-container-high`, elevation-2, `radius-md` 12, padding 12 × 16, width 280–320; title in title-small (14/20, `on-surface`), body in body-medium (`on-surface-variant`), up to 2 text actions in `primary`.
- **G · AI rich** — explains AI content: the rich format with the AI chip (`ai-container` + sparkle) before the title; the first action is the AI tonal button (“Ask the assistant”). Teal appears only because the content is AI.
- **Choose:** icon without a label → A (B if it has a shortcut); text cut with an ellipsis → C; a status needs its cause or time → D; a disabled control → E; a term needs a sentence or a link → F; AI-produced content → G. Anything longer, or anything to fill in → a menu, side sheet or dialog.
- **Timing:** plain after 500 ms of hover, immediately on keyboard focus; moving to the next icon within 300 ms skips the delay; hides 150 ms after the pointer leaves. Rich after 500 ms of hover or on click of the info icon, stays while the anchor or the tooltip is hovered or focused, 300 ms grace.
- **Placement:** 8 px from the anchor, centred; below in the header and the top 120 px of the screen, above elsewhere; flips on collision and keeps 8 px from the viewport edge (then aligns start or end). Rich: below-start of its info icon.
- **Motion:** enter fade + scale .8 → 1 from the anchor side, 150 ms, emphasized decelerate; exit fade 75 ms; reduced motion: fade only.
- **Accessibility:** `role="tooltip"` linked with `aria-describedby` (icon buttons keep their `aria-label`); Esc closes without moving focus; hovering the tooltip keeps it open (WCAG 1.4.13); rich tooltips with actions open on click and take focus. Contrast ≥ 7 : 1 everywhere: plain text 11.6 / 10.2, “Copied” 10.2 / 7.2, status tile 10.1 / 7.2, rich body 8.1 / 8.5, rich action 9.6 / 8.4 (light / dark).
- **Touch:** no hover — plain tooltips show on long-press for 1.5 s; rich tooltips open on tap of the info icon and close on tap outside; data tooltips become a “Copied” snackbar.
- **Don't:** repeat a visible label; put links, buttons or fields in a plain tooltip; hide information that exists nowhere else; use teal for a non-AI tooltip or a status colour without its symbol.
- **Keyboard and screen reader:** shows on hover after 500 ms and at once on keyboard focus; stays open while the pointer is on it (WCAG 1.4.13); Esc dismisses it without moving focus; it is the anchor's `aria-describedby`; a disabled reason is also a persistent hidden description.
- **Angular Material:** `matTooltip` for A–E with `matTooltipShowDelay="500"`, `matTooltipHideDelay="150"`, `matTooltipPosition="below|above"`, `matTooltipClass` = `ait-tip-data` / `ait-tip-status`. M2: `--mdc-plain-tooltip-container-color` = `inverse-surface`; M3: `--mat-sys-inverse-surface`. `matTooltip` renders text only, so B–D with structure (keys, value + hint, state tile) use the `.ait-tip` markup in a `cdkConnectedOverlay` with the same delays; F–G are an `ait-rich-tooltip` component in a `cdkConnectedOverlay`. Full map and theme files: `angular-material.md`.
