Ideas and Questions: user journey and implementation architecture

Community gets a Questions section that works like Stack Overflow (votes, accepted answers, tags, duplicates), tied to the Ideas box: a question that shows a missing feature becomes an idea, an idea lists the questions behind it, and a delivered idea answers them. Built on the Community feature, the domain reference and the Discussion rules.

Draft · 2026-11-26 · reopens the design-system decision "no votes, no accepted answer" (Discussion), see section 6.

1. User journey

Four roles: the person who asks, the people who answer (anyone, with champions on call for their tags), the people who vote on ideas, and the platform team who triages and delivers.

Ask and get an answer

  1. Ask from Community › Questions, from the header field ("Ask the community" when the assistant has no grounded answer), or from a page with its context attached.
  2. While the title is typed, similar questions appear, solved first. The assistant drafts an answer from Docs, marked as not checked.
  3. If one of them answers it, the person follows that question and nothing is posted.
  4. Answers arrive; the asker accepts one. Accepted answers are indexed so the assistant can cite them.

Answer and keep it moving

  1. Anyone can answer and vote. Tags are AIToolbox areas and Applications from the Software Catalog; each tag has champions.
  2. Unanswered after 2 working days: the tag's champions get a Teams mention; after 2 more, it lands in Administration › Community › Feedback › Inbox.
  3. No accepted answer after 7 days: the platform team can accept the best-voted one.

From question to idea and back

  1. "Propose as idea" on a question (or on an answer that says it cannot be done) opens the idea form prefilled; the question shows "Waiting on idea".
  2. Another question with the same need links to the idea (+1 demand), not a new idea.
  3. The team triages: merge duplicates, set the status, write the public note; Planned creates the Jira epic.
  4. When the release ships, every linked question gets a drafted answer ("Delivered in 4.8, here is how") that the team confirms, and followers are told.

2. Lifecycles and how they connect

A question and an idea each have their own status. The link between them is a record of its own, so one idea can carry many questions.

3. Implementation architecture

One new backend module, Community (CTX-COM), in the Devtoolbox backend. Postgres holds everything, search included, for v1; the assistant brings similar-question detection and draft answers; Jira and Teams go through CTX-INT as every other connector does.

Write path

  1. Every write (question, answer, vote, accept, idea, link) is one Postgres transaction that also inserts an outbox row.
  2. The relay publishes the event; the Notifier and CTX-INT react (Teams mention, Jira epic), CTX-KI ingests solved questions.
  3. Embeddings are computed after the commit, from the event, so a slow model never blocks a post.

Similar questions

  1. Debounced at 400 ms on the title: embed the text, then ORDER BY embedding <=> :q limited to 20, re-ranked with ts_rank on the title and body.
  2. Show 5, solved first, with their score and status.
  3. Duplicate closing uses the same query in the moderation view.

Scheduled jobs

  1. Every hour: questions over 2 working days without an answer → tag champions; over 4 → Feedback inbox (source "Questions").
  2. Daily: no accepted answer after 7 days → listed for the team.
  3. Weekly: Teams digest per tag (unanswered, solved, ideas that moved).

5. Rules that keep it healthy

  • Votes without points. Votes sort answers and questions; there is no reputation score and no badge in v1 (principle 6, minimal by default). Role chips stay as in Discussion: Platform team, Champion, Author.
  • The asker accepts. After 7 days without acceptance, the platform team can accept the best-voted answer; the page says who accepted it.
  • The assistant never posts on its own. Its draft shows on the Ask form and, once a person confirms it, as an answer marked "Drafted by the assistant, checked by …".
  • Bugs go to Jira. "Report a bug" on the Ask form and "Closed · bug in Jira" for questions that turn out to be bugs, as the Feedback rule says.
  • Everything is under the company SSO. No anonymous posting; names are shown. Moderation: report, hide, close as duplicate, move to an idea.

6. Decisions this reopens

  • Discussion rule. The design system says Discussion has "no votes, no same question, no accepted answer" and replaced the Q&A pattern. Proposal: keep Discussion as it is on releases; add Questions as a separate Community section with votes and accepted answers; on an idea, the linked questions replace the Discussion panel.
  • Vision consistency review. Its option A (simple Community) drops discussions. This proposal goes the other way and needs that choice made first.
  • Community navigation. What's new · Guides · Questions · Ideas.
  • Search store. Postgres full-text and pgvector for v1, a few thousand questions. Move to OpenSearch (already used for usage metrics) past about 50,000 posts or when search must cover Docs too.
